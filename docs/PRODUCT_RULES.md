# Agreed LuMaCo product rules

## Core purpose

Automatically maintain B2B relationships without requiring recurring manual CRM updates. After initial contact approval and integration authorization, keep follow-ups, commitments and customer context as up to date as permitted by connected sources. Be transparent about incomplete coverage.

## Brand & interface

Navy & Champagne Gold; dark navy header/sidebar, mostly white dashboard, balanced information density, gold LMC brand mark. **Needs Attention** is full width first; **Open Promises** comes immediately beneath; Smart Email follows. Company names and email sender names must be readable on light backgrounds.

## Contact admission and scope

All connected emails may be sorted by relationship and action category. **Only explicitly approved LuMaCo contacts** may create relationship history, promises or detailed reports. Unapproved personal emails must not become tracked contacts. Suggested Contacts is opt-in: preview, approve, dismiss and disable controls. Starred contacts get detailed reports by default; non-starred contacts can be manually opted in.

Contacts can have multiple labels: Engineer, Contractor, Vendor, Owner, Coworker and user-added custom categories. Identity matching may include multiple verified emails and numbers; ambiguous matches require approval. Companies and contacts are separate entities and may link to multiple projects.

## Separate outreach vs engagement

- Last Outreach: substantive, personalized outbound communication (or other qualifying outreach). Resets routine outreach follow-up cadence.
- Last Engagement: meaningful two-way interaction; tracked separately to show if the customer actually responded/engaged.
- Prolonged lack of engagement triggers a **separate** Needs Attention alert, even if outreach is current.
- Missed calls and connected calls of **2:00 or less** do **not** reset meaningful engagement. A **connected call longer than 2:00** qualifies.
- Routine automated emails and short 'thanks' acknowledgements should not reset relationship follow-ups.
- Meaningful text and email exchanges can qualify; sender approval and available connector data are required.
- Outreach and engagement reminder frequencies should be independently configurable per contact.
- Calendar events (client lunch, coffee, site visit, dinner, meeting) can qualify as engagement if matched to an approved contact and not canceled. An ended calendar event is **inferred**, not proof of attendance; provide confirm/reject/undo.

## Open Promises and attention

Maintain the exact label **Open Promises**. Extract commitments from approved sources automatically when justified, with source, owner, due date, and status; avoid duplicates and support changes/closure/undo. Needs Attention is a curated action queue of overdue outreach, lack of engagement, unanswered messages, upcoming commitments, drafts, or other verified urgent needs. The same promise should not dominate both sections without adding new value.

## Meetings and capture

Before Your Meeting briefings show approved last discussions, projects, promises and suggested talking points. After a qualifying call with a detailed contact, offer quick notes. After a scheduled client meeting, offer participant-consented live recording or a post-meeting verbal recap. Save reviewed text; do not permanently retain audio by default. Real microphone, OS and consent requirements remain future work.

## Manager view

One platform, different salesperson and manager roles. Managers get company-authorized account summaries, outreach/engagement status, team and individual progress, shared Open Promises and assignments. Salesforce-style manual logging should be unnecessary when integration coverage permits, but not all real-world interactions are observable. Managers must see which sources are connected and which insights are inferred. Personal and private notes must never leak through reports or metrics; company business-record retention rules still apply.

## Honest prototype boundaries

The current frontend runs **simulations** for email, SMS, phone, calendar, meeting transcription and AI. The local backend is separate and non-authenticated. Zero manual entry is a product **goal**, not an achieved production guarantee.
